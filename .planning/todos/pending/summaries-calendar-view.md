## Problem

Summaries page currently only shows a chronological list view. Users may prefer a calendar-based view to quickly see which days have summaries, spot gaps, and navigate by date. There's no way to visually scan an entire month at a glance.

## Solution

Add a view toggle (list/calendar) to the Summaries page header. Calendar view shows a month grid where each day cell indicates whether a summary exists (with visual cues like dot/color). Clicking a day navigates to that day's summary detail. Days without summaries are visually distinct. Navigation arrows to switch months.
